Evaluation Tool

Verification Checklist

A practical tool for evaluating any Bitcoin-based system against the constitutional requirements. Check each item to track your evaluation. This checklist does not create binding obligations — it is a convenience tool.

How to use: Check each item as you verify it against the system being evaluated. Items are organized by constitutional topic. References to specific articles are provided for each item. This checklist is interactive — your checks are saved in your browser session only.
